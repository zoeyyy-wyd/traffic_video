# --- vehicle position ---------------------------------------------------------
# EVERY LINE NAMES THE PARTY AT RISK, and none names a signal. The two are
# alternative ways to cut out the meaningless majority, and this is the better
# one. A vehicle on the crosswalk markings under a red hand obstructs nobody,
# and the red hand covers most of a cycle, so an unconditioned query spends the
# model's handful of returned matches on instances that carry no information.
#
# The obvious fix -- condition on the signal -- makes the model do the one
# thing measurement shows it is worst at. Filtering by the measured phase after
# the fact does not work either: the model returns a selected list, not an
# exhaustive one, so a filter that removes everything cannot distinguish "no
# such event" from "it reported the wrong ones and the real one was crowded
# out". The filter destroys recall.
#
# Naming the affected party instead -- pedestrians present, a cyclist going
# straight, vehicles moving through the crosswalk -- focuses the model on the
# instances that matter, needs no signal, and is visible in the frames. The
# measured phase timeline is then a free cross-check at scoring time rather
# than the thing the answer depends on.
