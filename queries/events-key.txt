# Event categories, one per line:  name | description.
#
# A query has to be precise enough that two people labelling the clip
# independently would mark the same instances, and it should take more than an
# object detector to answer. Queries that failed one of those tests were
# removed; the reasons live in README section 4.
#
# "Not present" is a correct answer. An instance that fits two categories is
# recorded under both.

turn-halt      | a vehicle that stops, or almost stops, part-way through a turn; the interval is the halt itself, from the vehicle stopping to it moving off
pickup-car     | a person getting into or out of a stopped car, van or taxi
bus-dwell      | a bus stopped at the kerb with people getting on or off it; the interval is the whole stop, from the bus halting to it pulling away

ped-midblock   | a pedestrian crossing the roadway away from the crosswalk markings, from stepping off the kerb until reaching the far side
# a skateboard, not an e-scooter or kick-scooter (those have handlebars)
skateboard     | a person riding a skateboard

# calibration: visible for the whole clip, so a wrong answer here means
# something is broken upstream of the query
roadworks      | the roadway is under construction: cones, barriers, an excavation, or workers standing in the carriageway

# a pair about one vehicle: a police car does pass, and exactly one of these is
# true of it. Answering "present" to both means the vehicle is being matched
# without its state being read.
emergency      | an emergency vehicle with its lights flashing passing through the intersection
emergency-dark | an emergency vehicle passing through the intersection with its lights NOT flashing

# negatives: absence established, no lookalike in the scene
negative | two vehicles making contact with each other
negative | a horse-drawn carriage
